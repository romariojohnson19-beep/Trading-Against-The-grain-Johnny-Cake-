FAQ

Questions.

Will this help me pass my prop firm eval? +

That's the goal. Most traders fail evals not because they lack a strategy — but because they've never seen real execution under pressure. Watching Mario trade live on NQ shows you what controlled execution looks like under pressure. No result is guaranteed.

Is this coaching or financial advice? +

Neither. Wingman is a live trade room — Mario trades live and members watch, observe, and ask questions in real time. This is not financial advice, investment advice, or coaching. It's access to a live trader's process, for educational purposes only.

What platform do live sessions run on? +

Discord Stage. Invite link sent on purchase. No extra apps needed beyond Discord.

What if I join mid-week? +

Weekly seats start Monday. Mid-week, grab a Day Pass for the remaining sessions that week, then roll into a weekly seat the following Monday.

Is the book included with Wingman Weekly? +

The book is a separate purchase. Most members grab it first.

What markets does the room cover? +

Primarily NQ futures. The Johnny Cake Method applies broadly — but live sessions are built around NQ.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. No contracts and no lock-ins.

What happens on a Day Pass if no trades fire? +

Full refund or rollover to the next session, your call.

How many people are in the room? +

12 seats each week, hard cap. Seats are billed weekly, so you can step in or out week to week.
