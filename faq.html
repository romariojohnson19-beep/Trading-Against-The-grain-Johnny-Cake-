Pricing and billing

How much does Wingman cost?

Wingman is $200 per week for a seat in the live room, which runs Monday to Thursday. That works out to about $50 per live session. Prices are in US dollars. Full details are on the pricing page.

How much is the book?

Trading the Grain is a one-time $47 purchase with instant access.

How does weekly billing work?

Your card is charged each week while you hold a seat. Payments are handled by Stripe, so card details never touch this site.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel before the next week starts and you will not be billed for it. Use the billing link in your member dashboard or email Mario.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund requests are handled case by case. Email Mario with your purchase details and he will reply directly. Please do not assume a guarantee that is not written here.

Where do I enter a promo code?

At checkout, in the “Add promotion code” box when it is shown. Current codes appear on the pricing page and in the occasional site pop-up. A code only works on the products it applies to.

How much is the Day Pass or Bootcamp?

Mario confirms the Day Pass price when he confirms your slot. Bootcamp pricing is shared with waitlist members when a spot opens.

What you get

Are these trade signals?

No. Wingman is a live trade room for education. Mario shows how he plans and trades a session. You make your own decisions with your own money.

Do you guarantee I will pass a funded-account evaluation or be profitable?

No. Nobody can. Trading carries substantial risk of loss and many people lose money. Anything shown in the room is an example, not a promise.

Why only 12 seats a week?

Small groups let questions get answered. When the room is full, new people go on the next week or the waitlist.

Are sessions recorded?

Replays are being added to the member dashboard as they become available. Do not buy expecting a full replay library on day one.

What markets does Mario trade?

Mainly Micro E-mini Nasdaq futures (MNQ), with other index futures as he notes in the room.

Account and privacy

Do I need an account?

Yes, for Wingman. An account gives you the member dashboard. The book does not need one.

How is my data handled?

See the privacy policy. Your data is used to run your account and is not sold.

I forgot my password.

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in form and you will get a reset email.
