The universe

Welcome to
the Cake Market.

The bulls and bears bake all day. Every cake is a stash of orders left at a level they plan to come back to. Johnny and Dæv make their living off those cakes. One waits for his slice. The other doesn’t.

The crew

Johnny Crow

A Jamaican John Crow. He watches the bulls and bears bake, lets the cake come back to market, and only takes his slice once price proves it. Pays fair, eats late, rarely gets burned. Runs the live room.

The lore

Johnny cakes

The spot price launched from, with unfilled orders still sitting there. On your chart: a supply or demand zone.

Hot and cold cakes

A fresh cake is hot. Every time price comes back, someone takes a bite. Go back too often and there’s nothing left.

The golden window

A zone Johnny waits for price to return to. Dæv’s usually already there.
