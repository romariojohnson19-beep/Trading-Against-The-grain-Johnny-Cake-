Neither. Wingman is a live trade room — Mario trades live and members watch, observe, and ask questions in real time. This is not financial advice, investment advice, or coaching. It's access to a live trader's process, for educational purposes only.

What platform do live sessions run on? +

Discord Stage. Invite link sent on purchase. No extra apps needed beyond Discord.

What if I join mid-week? +

Weekly seats lock in on Monday. Mid-week, grab a Day Pass for any remaining sessions that week — then roll into a weekly seat from the following Monday.

Is the book included with Wingman Weekly? +

Not included by default — the book is a separate purchase. Most members grab it first. DM Mario about member discounts.

What markets does the room cover? +

Primarily NQ futures. The Johnny Cake Method applies broadly — but live sessions are built around NQ.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. No contracts. No lock-ins. Stay because it's working.

What happens on a Day Pass if no trades fire? +

12 weekly seats. Hard cap, no exceptions. Mario knows everyone in the room.
