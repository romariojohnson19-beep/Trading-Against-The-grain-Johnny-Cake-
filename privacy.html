Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: name, email, optional Discord handle.
  • Requests you submit, such as waitlist or Day Pass forms.
  • Basic usage needed to run the member dashboard.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.

How we use it

To run your account, show your schedule and files, reply to you and send the updates you signed up for. We do not sell your personal data.

Who handles it

Hosting is by Vercel, data storage and sign-in by Supabase, and payments by Stripe. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing the address below. You can also leave at any time.

Cookies and storage

The site uses your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and remember settings such as a dismissed promotion. It does not use advertising trackers.
